### 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Resolute Data Protection, LLC (“Resolute,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in connection with the Resolute Data Protection informational website.

This Policy applies only to the website. It does not govern information handled under a separate consulting, expert, research, training, advisory, or other professional engagement. Any separate engagement may be governed by additional agreements, notices, confidentiality obligations, or data-handling terms.

### 2. Current Website Status

The website is currently an informational website hosted through GitHub Pages.

The current contact form is a demonstration interface. In its present configuration, the form validates entries in the visitor’s browser but does not transmit the information to Resolute, an email inbox, a form-processing provider, or a database.

Before the form is activated, this Policy must be updated to identify the actual form processor, recipient or recipients, purposes of use, retention period, and other applicable practices.

### 3. Information Processed When You Visit the Website

#### 3.1 Information processed by the hosting provider

GitHub Pages hosts the website. GitHub states that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor’s Internet Protocol address is logged and stored for security purposes. GitHub may also process technical and usage information in accordance with its own privacy statement.

Resolute does not currently receive a visitor analytics dashboard or application-level visitor profile from the website code.

#### 3.2 Information stored in your browser session

The website contains interactive features that may temporarily hold selections or text in browser memory while a page is open. This information is not currently transmitted to Resolute and is generally cleared when the page is refreshed, closed, or the interface is reset.

A service selected through the website’s guidance interface may appear in the page URL. URLs may be retained in browser history or shared if a visitor copies the URL.

#### 3.3 Information you may provide through the contact form

The contact form currently displays fields for:

- Name
- Email address
- Organization, which is optional
- Service interest
- Message

At present, submitting the form does not deliver this information to Resolute. If the form is activated later, this section must be revised before activation to accurately describe what is collected, where it is sent, who may access it, and how long it is retained.

### 4. Cookies, Analytics, and Advertising

The current website code does not use:

- Application-level analytics
- Advertising pixels
- Behavioral tracking
- Marketing cookies
- Local storage
- Session storage
- Newsletter tracking
- Session-replay tools

GitHub may use cookies or other technologies in connection with its own services and infrastructure under GitHub’s own policies. Resolute does not control GitHub’s independent processing.

If Resolute later adds analytics, cookies, advertising tools, embedded content, or similar technologies, this Policy will be updated before or when those features are activated, and any legally required consent mechanism will be considered.

### 5. External Links

The website may link to third-party websites, including LinkedIn.

When you follow an external link, the third party may receive information such as your Internet Protocol address, device and browser information, referring page, and information about your interaction with its service. The third party’s own privacy policy governs its processing.

Resolute is not responsible for the privacy, security, availability, or content of third-party websites.

### 6. How Information Will Be Used if the Contact Form Is Activated

Before activation, Resolute must confirm and approve the actual practices. The expected purposes may include:

- Responding to inquiries
- Understanding the requested service or business need
- Determining whether a consultation or engagement may be appropriate
- Scheduling or coordinating follow-up communications
- Maintaining business records
- Protecting the website and preventing misuse
- Complying with applicable legal obligations

Resolute will not use inquiry information for unrelated marketing unless that practice is separately disclosed and any required permission is obtained.

### 7. Sensitive, Confidential, or Privileged Information

Do not submit confidential, privileged, classified, incident-sensitive, health, financial, authentication, regulated, or other highly sensitive information through the website.

Submitting information through the website does not create:

- An attorney-client relationship
- A consultant-client relationship
- A professional engagement
- A duty of confidentiality beyond any duty imposed by applicable law
- Attorney-client privilege or another legal privilege

A formal professional relationship exists only after the relevant parties complete and accept a separate written agreement.

### 8. Service Providers and Disclosure of Information

The website currently relies on GitHub Pages for hosting.

Before activating the contact form, Resolute must identify the approved form processor and email provider. The final Policy should name or describe those providers and explain their roles.

Resolute may disclose information where reasonably necessary to:

- Operate and protect the website
- Use approved service providers
- Respond to a lawful request
- Protect rights, safety, security, or property
- Complete a transaction or organizational change, subject to applicable law
